1a:["$","p","8",{"children":"Since the fans will not push down all the heat, you want the rest of it to reflect back into the heat chamber. Any oven that utilizes a metal (hopefully stainless) heat shield will have a tremendously higher efficiency than exposed brick insulation. It also means if your heat shield is dirty or damaged, you can improve the oven efficiency if you clean and/or fix the heat shield. Although it may sound counter-intuitive, “too much” heat shield can actually lower your oven’s efficiency. By ” too much”, I am referring to the way the heat shield is installed. If all sides of the heat shield are attached to the oven frame, there will be too much metal-to-metal contact, and heat will just escape to the outside of the oven. Not only will you lose some efficiency, your oven shell can become quite hot and dangerous to touch. A cleverly designed oven can isolate the heat shield to minimize the metal-to metal contact."}]
