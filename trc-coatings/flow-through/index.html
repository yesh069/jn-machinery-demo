Flow-through systems (FTS)

For coating garage door springs or other long springs using minimal tank sizes. Springs come out dry to the touch in less than 30 seconds after stress relief, per JN.

01 Overview

What is FTS™?

The flow-through system uses the heat of the stress-relieving operation to apply a polymer conversion coating (Aqua-TRC) to formed springs at 350°F (177°C) or hotter. The entire tempering and painting process can be done in-house, in a small space, together with coiling, forming and stress relief.

Quote an FTS

FTS 12IN system. System length 131″, 36″ wide, adjustable height; 115 V, 5.3 A.

02 Process

Spray, don’t dip.

03 Engineering advantages

Benefits JN lists for FTS.

  • No splash, no dribbles, no waste: 100% of unused paint returns to the tank
  • Dries instantly: no drying conveyor needed, so lower machinery cost, electricity and production time
  • Springs can be striped immediately, reducing manual handling
  • Heat (steam) is released to the air rather than absorbed into the paint solution
  • Colour changes in under 10 minutes
  • Short runs are as efficient as long runs
  • Start each day with a fresh pail; top off with the previous day’s remainder

04 System components

Three working parts.

  • Tank

    Holds the coating material on casters and carries the pump; captures run-off through draining tubes.

  • Coating plates

    Deliver an Aqua-TRC liquid curtain. Height-adjustable, available in multiple diameters, with flow set by four adjustable valves.

  • Two-part conveyor

    12″ belt with speed control: an ingress belt from the oven and an egress belt to coning and striping.
