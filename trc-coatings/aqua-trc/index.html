Aqua-TRC® coatings

A concentrated water-based polymer coating material, specifically designed to overcome dispersion and settling shortcomings found in other water-based paints.

JN MACHINERY AT AN INDUSTRY TRADE SHOW · EXISTING JN PHOTOGRAPH

01 Overview

What is Aqua-TRC?

  • Concentrate designed to be diluted with water from 1:1 to 4:1 before use, formulated to coat hot materials
  • A polymer that converts (bonds) to a metal spring with no additional curing process
  • Environmentally safe, with no hazardous materials, and usable in open air without ventilation equipment (per JN)
  • Conversion coat: a smooth surface with little or no build-up under the end coils
  • Formulated by TRC Coatings on a patented polymer

02 Key features

How it behaves in production.

Statements below are JN’s, drawn from its Aqua-TRC FAQ and comparison pages.

  • Stays dispersed

    Uses “dispersed pigment” chemistry. Five to ten seconds of stirring is enough to agitate the liquids.

  • Doesn’t stick to machinery

    A non-sticky resin that will not stick to non-heated surfaces. JN says machinery self-cleans in 20 minutes.

  • Dries in seconds in TRC and FTS

    Further processing such as striping can be done immediately after coating. Full cure takes about 12 hours and happens naturally.

  • No ammonia handling

    JN states Aqua-TRC does not need ammonia to control odour or bacteria.

  • Wide operating temperature

    JN states it functions from 38°F to 191°F with no detrimental effect when the paint returns to room temperature.
