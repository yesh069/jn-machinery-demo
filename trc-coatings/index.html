Division B: TRC coating systems

An in-line process that uses the heat of the tempering and stress-relieving operation to apply a polymer conversion coating: corrosion protection and colour-coding in one pass.

01 What is TRC?

Tempering, Rustproofing and Color Coating.

TRC is designed to convey hot materials through a coating material and deposit dry-to-the-touch product in less than 3 minutes, says JN. The entire tempering and painting process can be done in-house together with standard coiling, forming and stress-relief processes.

Formed parts at 350°F (177°C) or hotter drop into a polymer solution. An incline conveyor removes them from the dip tank and drops them onto a drying conveyor. It is a compact in-line system that requires little to no additional labour.
