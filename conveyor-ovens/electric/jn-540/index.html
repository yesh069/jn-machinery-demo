01 Overview

A small oven, ideal for parts up to .062″ (2mm) wire

5″ (127mm) wide, turned-up belt edge. Solid body design with stainless steel conveyor. Automatic time delay shut down for belt cooling.

05 Applications

What this oven is used for.

  • Spring heat treatment and stress relieving

    Springs made from carbon steel, stainless steel or chrome silicon wire. JN's published studies argue that on-line, continuous heat treatment beats traditional batch ovens in most spring applications.

  • TRC coating

    Tempering, Rustproofing and Color Coating in one in-line process, using the heat from stress relieving to apply a polymer conversion coating.

  • Beyond springs

    JN states its ovens are also used for rustproofing, tempering metal, baking, oiling, drying and sterilization.
